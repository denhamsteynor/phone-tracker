Location Timeline

    Import Google Timeline

    Your file is read in this browser and saved straight to your own database. It isn't sent anywhere else.

    1. Newer phones: on your Android phone open Settings → Location → Location services → Timeline (or Google Maps → your picture → Your Timeline → ⋮ → Location & privacy settings) and tap Export Timeline data. It saves Timeline.json.
    2. Older Google Takeout exports also work: Records.json and the monthly files from Semantic Location History (select several at once).
    3. You can open this website on the phone and pick the file there, or copy it to your laptop.
